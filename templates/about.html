About The Project

Building Safer Roads Through Artificial Intelligence

Poor road conditions and potholes are major causes of vehicle damage, traffic congestion, and road accidents. Traditional road inspection methods require significant time and manual effort, making it difficult to identify road defects quickly.

This project presents an intelligent web-based solution that assists in improving road safety by automatically identifying damaged roads and supporting better traffic management. It aims to provide useful insights for both drivers and road maintenance authorities.

  • Improves road safety awareness
  • Supports timely road maintenance
  • Reduces risks caused by damaged roads
  • Provides an easy-to-use web platform
  • Contributes to smarter transportation systems
