System Methodology

Methodology

System Workflow

The system follows a structured pipeline to detect potholes, assess traffic conditions, and recommend safer travel routes.

Road Image Acquisition

The system accepts road images captured through the device camera or uploaded by the user for further analysis.

Core Feature

AI-Based Detection

The trained YOLOv8 model detects potholes, draws bounding boxes, and determines the total number of potholes present.

AI Detection

Risk Evaluation

Traffic density, weather conditions, and pothole count are analyzed together to estimate the overall road risk level.

Risk Assessment

Alternative Route Planning

When unsafe road conditions are detected, the system recommends a safer alternative route with travel information.

Smart Routing
How It Works

Processing Pipeline

The complete sequence followed by the system from receiving an image to generating the final analysis.

1

Image Acquisition

The user captures or uploads a road image and provides the required traffic information.

2

Pothole Detection

YOLOv8 processes the image and identifies potholes by generating bounding boxes and detection confidence scores.

3

Traffic & Weather Analysis

Traffic density, weather conditions, and pothole count are combined to estimate road safety.

4

Results Generation

The system displays the detected potholes, risk level, travel information, and recommended alternative route.

Traffic Risk

Road Risk Classification

Road conditions are categorized into three levels to help users understand travel safety.

Low Risk

Road is safe. No or very few potholes detected. Traffic can flow normally without major concerns.

0 Potholes / Low Traffic

Medium Risk

Proceed with caution. Some potholes detected. Drivers should slow down and stay alert.

1–3 Potholes / Medium Traffic

High Risk

Heavy road damage or congestion. Alternate route recommended for safer travel.

4+ Potholes / High Traffic
